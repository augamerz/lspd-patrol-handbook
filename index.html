BIA & Internal Affairs

Corruption is PROHIBITED! Corruption violates both LSPD values and Hydrate Roleplay rules. Officers must uphold honesty, fairness, and professionalism. Bribery, abuse of power, falsifying evidence, or any unethical conduct is strictly prohibited.

Internal Affairs investigates misconduct within the police force. Reports can be filed by officers or the public.

Bureau of Integrity and Accountability (BIA) is an external agency that handles complaints of government corruption, including LSPD, EMS, and DoJ. Reports can also be submitted by both staff of departments and civilians.

Report any suspected corrupt activities by fellow members of the force to the BIA immediately. A receptionist at the FIB Building (PC: 398) will take your complaints.

All reports will be handled with confidentiality and professionalism to ensure that the integrity of our police force is maintained.

Petrov Rights — Introduced: 14/03/2025

Charge TypeDescription
InfractionOn-the-spot fine. Once fined, offender is to be released on the spot.
MisdemeanorMinor charge. Can be either a fine and/or jail time (charge dependent).
FelonyMajor charge. Will always include jail time.

Processing Suspects — Part 1

  • Charges that result in jail time (many misdemeanors, all felonies): you are required to transport the offender to a police station.
  • Charges that do not result in jail time: offenders can be charged on scene and released.
  • If the offender does not have a photo in the MDT and is being charged with a felony, organize taking one at the station prior to sentencing. This is mandatory.

Open the Reports tab. Click New Report, then write a brief write-up of what happened (recommended for felonies). Select all involved officers and link any evidence lockers or photos taken.

This report will be saved for future reference, and officers can go back and look at evidence collected.

Processing Suspects — Part 2

  • Charges that result in jail time (many misdemeanors, all felonies): transport the offender to a police station.
  • Charges that do not result in jail time: charge on scene and release.
  1. In the Reports tab of the MDT, select + Add Criminals and choose the individual you wish to charge.
  2. Select charges using Edit Charges.
  3. Once all charges are set, sentence per sentencing guidelines (fine & jail criminal if the charge requires jail time).
  4. Select Process Criminal and pleaded guilty only if they admitted guilt.

When jailing an offender, they are teleported straight to prison. If you wish to transport by boat or helicopter, this is also an option and encouraged.

Department of Public Safety (DPS)

Highway Guidelines

Main responsibilities — On Highway patrol, our primary responsibility is attending crime scenes where a pursuit is likely (e.g. bank robbery). Secondary responsibilities include traffic stops, speeding infringements, random breath tests, and defective vehicles.

As Highway we lead by example with driving standards, pursuit comms, spacing (enforcing pursuit spacing when necessary), and making appropriate split-second decisions under pressure.

Authority Highway Corporals and above who are fully aware of the situation can call pursuits imperative. Highway Sergeants and above who are fully aware of the situation can approve PITs. Judgement and common sense must be applied to every situation. Command always have overruling authority. Failure to comply will result in disciplinary action.

Interceptor

  • Interceptors can only be taken out by interceptor-certified officers. You must be Senior Trooper+ to apply for Interceptor Cert. Every officer Lieutenant and below must pass a strict interceptor test.
  • Only 1 interceptor unit can be deployed for every 2 Highway units on duty, with a maximum of 2 interceptors at any given time.
  • Use common sense and judgement if an interceptor is necessary. If it is likely to be OP, fall back from primary, or run parallels or blocks.
  • Interceptor operators are expected to have the highest driving etiquette in the force.
  • If your interceptor becomes non-recoverable, you cannot take another out until tsunami.
Disciplinary Failure to adhere to the above will result in disciplinary action.

S.W.A.T Handbook

Hydrate Roleplay · Welcome to SWAT

Congratulations on joining SWAT. As members of this elite unit, your primary mission is to address high-risk and dangerous incidents — actively patrolling the city and responding to critical events such as shots fired, hostage situations, and other scenarios that exceed general patrol capabilities.

Your Role as SWAT

SWAT will be the aggressors during any scene and are well aware of their role. When attaching to a scene you should be on surrounding rooftops, staying mobile, and if possible an operator with the suspect(s) at all times.

  • Rooftop operators constantly relay information to the Team Leader / Scene Command so everyone is aware of surroundings.
  • Mobile operators scout vantage points and look for possible flanks on enemy combatants.
  • Vehicle occupancy — Maximum of three officers per patrol vehicle unless explicit approval for higher capacity is granted, or the vehicle is designed for more.
  • Weapon presentation — Only present your two-handed weapon when absolutely necessary. Avoid displaying it casually.
  • Use of force (class matching) — Match the level of force appropriately. Do not deploy a rifle against armed offenders with only a semi-automatic pistol.
  • Professional conduct — Always use common sense and maintain professional behaviour.
  • Accountability — Be prepared to justify all decisions if questioned by Command, Internal Affairs (IA & BIA), or the Department of Justice (DOJ).
  • Riot shield — Carry only during a breach or an operation, not for everyday carry.

Notes from Command

Do not payfarm SWAT members are hand-picked to lead, command, and enforce. You are the authority on scene and are expected to act fast, hard, and without apology.

Stand your ground and shut down anyone who steps out of line. Be tough, be firm, and make sure the public knows who’s in charge. No exceptions.

When SWAT shows up, take control with a tough, no-nonsense attitude. Team Leaders give orders, not requests, and expect immediate compliance. Members are approachable but maintain authority and expect quick action from the public. Handle non-compliance swiftly.

SWAT Lockboxes

SWAT members can store their weapons and tactical gear in the provided lockboxes. When accessing these items, officers must adhere to the standard deployment ratio.

This ensures balanced gameplay and prevents unfair advantages, unless the department is facing an “all hands on deck” situation.

High-Risk Threats

Armed suspects inside buildings or compounds — Interior holds; execution of raids/search warrants on known organised groups.

Organised & coordinated crime — Criminal groups engaging in bank robberies, major heists, or mass shootouts; drug lab raids; warehouse operations.

Officer safety & public risk — Responding officers outnumbered, outgunned, or trapped; threats or coordinated attacks on government/police property; extraction of police from active hostile/ambush zones or hostage recoveries.

Hostage Situations

A hostage situation involves individual(s) being held captive against their will. There are three general types:

  • Police station vicinity — Where an officer is held at or around a station: lethal force is authorised and recommended (not instantly — use discretion and follow scene command).
  • EMS hostage — Lethal force is authorised and recommended (not instantly — use discretion and follow scene command).
  • Police officer hostage — Non-lethal force is preferred. Use common sense, relay critical information to scene command, support negotiations, initiate pursuits, or be ready with lethal/non-lethal options to regain control.

Full SOPs are found in the SOPs section of the MDT.

Activation only Only use your SWAT weaponry when you’re officially activated as a SWAT unit.

If an “all hands on deck” situation arises while you’re on general patrol, you must change into your SWAT uniform and use your judgment for that single scene. Once the situation is Code 4, return to your previous division.

Operational Procedures & Discipline

Being a police officer on Hydrate is a privilege, and serving in the elite SWAT division is an even greater one. You will be held to an exceptionally high standard of professionalism.

Disciplinary actions Violations of any policies/rules in this handbook or a failure to follow orders will result in disciplinary action.

SWAT Activation vs Patrolling

SWAT Patrol

  • You must be on the SWAT job role.
  • Primary responsibility: remain mobile, respond quickly, and be one of the first operators on scene for any situation requiring SWAT.
  • While on patrol, you are required to be in approved SWAT vehicles at all times.

SWAT Activation

  • You are not required to be on the SWAT job role.
  • If a situation escalates and SWAT is called, you may pull your SWAT-issued weapons immediately.
  • At the first safe and available opportunity, switch into full SWAT uniform.
  • You may use your current vehicle — no need to change to your SWAT car.

Triage

A triage location is a designated, defensive location where people can safely receive more advanced medical attention after a high-profile shootout. They prevent a continuous cycle of violence where reinforcements arrive and open fire trying to rescue fellow criminals.

Why location is everything

  • Minimising threat — Choose a spot with minimal rooftops, or rooftops that can be held by police, so the opposing side cannot easily gain high ground.
  • Controlling the environment — A good location funnels the enemy into predictable lines of fire and allows superior cover for police and EMS.
